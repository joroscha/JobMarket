# Spotify Recruiter Call Questions

## Immigration: Green Card / PERM

1. What is Spotify's policy on employment-based green card sponsorship?

2. When would Spotify typically initiate the PERM/I-140 process after start date?

3. Does Spotify start the green card process while someone is on OPT/STEM OPT, or usually only after H-1B?

4. Is there a minimum tenure or performance review period before starting PERM?

5. Once PERM/I-140 starts, what are the major steps and what should I expect timeline-wise?

## Relocation / Work Location

1. If I move to NYC, Hoboken, or Jersey City in August, who should I notify and how much advance notice does Spotify need for address/work-location changes?

2. If I live in New Jersey but use the NYC office regularly, how does Spotify handle work location and payroll tax withholding?

3. Would living in New Jersey affect anything about office access, benefits, or employment setup?

4. Is there any relocation or transition support available for a voluntary move closer to the office, or is that only available when Spotify requires a move?

5. Are there any internal resources for employees relocating to the NYC area, such as neighborhood guidance, relocation vendors, or employee groups?

## Onboarding, If Time

1. I've already started receiving onboarding tasks in Workday. Are there any specific deadlines or dependencies I should be aware of before my start date?

2. When should I expect benefits enrollment to open?

3. When should I expect equipment setup/shipping details?

## Benefits, If Time

1. When do medical, dental, and vision benefits begin?

2. When do I need to make benefit elections?

3. Can you confirm whether employee contributions for medical/dental/vision are pre-tax?
